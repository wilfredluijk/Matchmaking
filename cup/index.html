      Rules & handicap reference

      Format

      • Two teams of four players each: A1–A4 and B1–B4.
      • 18 matches total: 16 singles + 2 doubles.
      • Sets are best of 5 games to 11 points. Each won set = 1 point.
      • Two tables in parallel: odd match number → Table 1, even → Table 2.

      Match order

      1. A1–B3 · A2–B4 · A3–B1 · A4–B2
      2. A1–B4 · A2–B3 · A3–B2 · A4–B1
      3. Doubles A1+A2 vs B3+B4
      4. Doubles A3+A4 vs B1+B2
      5. A1–B2 · A2–B1 · A3–B4 · A4–B3
      6. A1–B1 · A2–B2 · A3–B3 · A4–B4

      Doubles handicap

      Two doubles are played: A1+A2 vs B3+B4 and A3+A4 vs B1+B2. Each doubles' head start is computed on its own two boards:

      • Take the head start for the highest-rated player of each team (top board).
      • Take the head start for the lowest-rated player of each team (bottom board).
      • Add the two and divide by 2, then round the decimal up (ceil of the absolute value).
      • The weaker side of that average receives the head start.
